Privacy Policy — Dokopi

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Developer, and controller of your data under the GDPR: nextGenDev, India
Contact: nextgendevls@gmail.com

Dokopi is a PDF scanner and editor for Android, available on Google Play under the package name com.nextgendev.dokopi. This policy explains what happens to your documents and what data the app and the services it uses collect.

Your documents stay on your device

  • Documents you scan, import, create or edit are stored in Dokopi's private storage on your phone. Dokopi doesn't upload them anywhere and has no server that receives them.
  • Scanning uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, which runs in Google Play services on your phone. Dokopi itself doesn't use the camera; the scanner shows its own camera screen and processes the images on the device.
  • Text recognition (OCR) uses Google's ML Kit text recognition model, which is included in the app and runs on the device.
  • Scanning QR codes and barcodes uses Google's code scanner, which also runs in Google Play services on your phone and shows its own camera screen. Dokopi receives only the code's text, and opens a link, a payment app or anything else only when you tap to.
  • Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. It writes the sound of the sentence being read, and of the next one, to temporary files inside Dokopi, which other apps can't open. They are deleted once played, when reading is paused and you leave Read aloud, or at the latest the next time you use it.
  • PDF to Word writes the Word file inside Dokopi until you save or share it; the next one replaces it.
  • Copies you choose to make: "Save to device" writes a copy where you pick, and "Also save to Downloads" (off by default) copies new and changed documents to Downloads/Dokopi, where other apps on your phone can open them.
  • Android backup: if backup is on for your phone, Android may include your Dokopi settings, and your documents when the backup is end-to-end encrypted (a phone with a screen lock), in your device backup to your Google account. Unsaved editor drafts and your saved signature are never included.
  • Uninstalling Dokopi deletes the documents stored inside it. Copies you saved elsewhere stay.

Ads and consent

The free version shows ads: a banner on the Files list, now and then a full-screen ad after a document is saved, and a video ad when you choose to watch one to use a Premium tool once. There are no ads while you scan or edit, and none at all with Premium. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), Dokopi asks for your consent before ads may use your data for personalization; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what data about you we hold, and to correct or delete it. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you also have the rights to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting what happened before), and to complain to your data protection authority.

Dokopi has no accounts, so we can't find data by your name or email. Most of it is tied to identifiers on your phone: deleting the app's data or reinstalling it gives Analytics a new app instance ID, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. For anything else, write to nextgendevls@gmail.com and we'll help.

International transfers

Google may process the data its services collect in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or Texas):

  • We collected, in the past 12 months, the categories in the table above: identifiers (advertising ID, app instance ID), internet or app activity, approximate location derived from the IP address, and device information.
  • We don't sell personal information for money. Showing personalized ads in the free version may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under these laws. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy choices where it's shown, by deleting your advertising ID, or with Premium, which has no ads.

Notifications

Dokopi may show:

  • a reminder two days before a free trial of Premium ends, if you agree when the trial starts (it's scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to a server);
  • a media notification while a document is being read aloud.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): for the trial reminder and the Read aloud controls.
  • Storage (Android 9 only): to save copies to the Downloads and Pictures folders.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep reading aloud with the screen off.

Children

Dokopi isn't directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA) and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Data inside the app is deleted when you uninstall it.
  • Data held by Google's services is kept and deleted under Google's policies (policies.google.com/privacy). Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days.
  • To ask for deletion of data linked to your app instance, write to nextgendevls@gmail.com.

Security

Data the services send travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Password protection in Dokopi uses AES-256.
